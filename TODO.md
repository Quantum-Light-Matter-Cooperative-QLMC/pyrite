# Debye-Waller provenance and anisotropy audit

Audit every catalog `B_ang2` against a temperature- and phase-specific primary
crystallographic source, replacing placeholders only when the reported ADP
convention and conversion are recoverable. Record each accepted value in the
physics-validation ledger, quantify scalar-model limits, then decide whether
evidence requires atom-specific isotropic or Cartesian tensor displacement
parameters before changing catalog schema.

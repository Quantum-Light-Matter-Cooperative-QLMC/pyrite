# Inelastic scattering and energy-loss straggling

`inelastic_model="continuous"` removes the full [SBETHE collision stopping](stopping-power.md) along each electron flight. `inelastic_model="shell-soft-hard"`, which production runs select by default wherever the shell model covers every layer ([configuration](shell-soft-hard-transport.md#configuration)), resolves large transfers as individual collisions while retaining small transfers as continuous loss. It is a PENELOPE-2024 shell generalized-oscillator-strength (GOS) model{cite:p}`salvat2024penelope`, closed to the same SBETHE mean. This page describes what a hard collision changes in a track and how fluctuations are assigned. The [shell soft/hard transport reference](shell-soft-hard-transport.md) gives the channel construction, sampling rules, and secondary cascade in detail.

## One collision-stopping budget

For each material, the shell GOS model combines distant longitudinal and transverse excitations, close Møller collisions, and EEDL inner-shell rates. An outer-shell factor closes its total first energy-loss moment to the layer's corrected SBETHE stopping magnitude $S(E)$. A transfer cutoff $W_c$ partitions the same model: $W\le W_c$ contributes to soft loss, and $W>W_c$ becomes a hard-event channel. At every SBETHE table node, the soft stopping $S_s$ plus the mean hard loss per length equals $S$. Equation {eq}`eq-shell-soft-hard-tables` defines the tabulated soft stopping, channel rates, and soft variance rate. The partition replaces continuous collision stopping; its two sides are never added to an unrestricted SBETHE loss.

The cutoff must exceed each layer's conduction-band resonance. Each layer needs a catalog shell model and an SBETHE table on the same energy nodes. The mode requires `energy_model="midpoint"`; grooved transport and the CUDA transport LUT do not support it. Validation: `penelope-shell-rate-closure`, `penelope-shell-soft-hard-partition`, `shell-soft-hard-transport`.

## Hard collision event

An independent hard-event optical depth competes with the elastic collision distance. At a hard event, the code chooses an oscillator and branch according to their rates, samples a transfer $W>W_c$ from that channel's restricted distribution, then samples the primary recoil angle and a uniform azimuth. The event ends the current physical flight. Its row records `hard_W_keV`, `hard_channel`, and `EVENT_HARD_INELASTIC`; the next flight begins at the same position and time with primary kinetic energy reduced by $W$ and a recoiled direction. If the transfer brings the primary to its cutoff, the event is recorded on a terminal `CUTOFF` row. Soft loss accrued along the preceding row is separate from the discrete transfer.

The transfer is allocated to local kinetic-energy deposition, a possible secondary electron, and reserved inner-shell binding. With `secondary_threshold_eV=None`, secondaries are not followed. With a threshold, a launched electron starts at the collision point and time, inherits the primary-history identity, and can produce later generations. Explicit vacancies reserve binding energy but do not yet emit characteristic photons; the characteristic estimator instead scores each transported track's path. See [secondary electron transport](shell-soft-hard-transport.md#secondary-electron-transport). Validation: `penelope-shell-hard-loss-sampling`, `penelope-shell-hard-recoil`, `shell-secondary-transport`.

## Role and type of straggling

`straggling=False` applies the mean soft loss along each row. With `straggling=True`, this mode fluctuates **only the soft part** using PENELOPE-2024's two-moment distribution{cite:p}`salvat2024penelope`: its mean is $m=S_s(E)s$ and its variance is $v=\Omega_s^2(E)s$, for row length $s$. The sampler uses a truncated Gaussian when $m^2>9v$, a uniform law when $3v<m^2\le9v$, and a point mass at zero plus a uniform tail when $m^2\le3v$. It returns a nonnegative loss and reverts to the mean when the variance vanishes. The hard-transfer tail is already represented by sampled collisions, so the unrestricted Urban sampler would count it twice.

The continuous mode instead uses the **unrestricted Urban compound-Poisson straggling** around the full SBETHE mean when `straggling=True`; it samples loss fluctuations without explicit hard collisions or secondary tracks. Its per-element Urban channels retain their historical shapes and receive a shared scale factor so their summed mean equals the material SBETHE stopping rate. The [electron transport reference](electron-transport.md#stochastic-energy-loss) describes the excitation and continuum components. These two straggling laws serve different partitions of the collision loss and are selected by `inelastic_model`. The soft GOS distribution is evaluated at the row-start energy; `max_dE_frac` limits energy drift within a row. Validation: `energy-loss-straggling`, `shell-soft-hard-transport`.

The separate [hard BremsLib photon mode](../radiation-physics/hard-bremsstrahlung-events.md) may run with either collision mode and optional straggling. The collision loss is sampled by that mode's law, while the BremsLib soft radiative part is added at its mean. Sampled hard inelastic transfers and hard photons debit energy as separate events. Validation: `bremslib-radiative-partition`.

## Scope and evidence

The shell mode runs on the exact CPU and CUDA cores and the CPU transport LUT. Its channel distributions use the row-start energy; the PENELOPE energy-dependence correction within a flight is not applied. The [validation ledger](../../validation/physics-validation-ledger.md) retains the status and evidence for the claims above. The `shell-soft-hard-transport` transport-level claim remains `unverified`; this description does not change that status.

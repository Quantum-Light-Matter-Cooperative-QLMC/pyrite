## `energy-controlled-propagation`

- **Claim:** `max_dE_frac=f` (which requires `energy_model="midpoint"`) splits a physical flight using the deterministic left-endpoint control `s_cap=f E_start/C(E_start)`. This bounds the predicted mean loss, not the realized midpoint or Urban loss. The elastic collision is drawn ONCE per physical flight as an optical depth `tau = -ln(U)` and consumed across the flight's substeps at each substep's own hazard, `tau -= ds/lambda(E_substep)`; the flight closes when the budget is exhausted, at a boundary, at the cutoff, or on termination.
